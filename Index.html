Getting Started

🧭 Dashboard Overview

The Play Ops Dashboard is your central hub for all team activity. Use the left sidebar to navigate between views. Your current points, rank, and streak are always visible on your Profile page. Managers have additional tabs under the Manager section.

🔔 Notifications

The bell icon in the top-right shows unread alerts. You'll be notified when a task is assigned to you specifically, when your Kudos submission is approved or rejected, when you earn a badge, and when demerits are added to your record.

🌙 Dark Mode & Sidebar

Toggle dark mode using the 🌙 Dark button in the top header — your preference saves automatically. Collapse the sidebar with the ☰ icon for more screen space. Your session and role determine which tabs are visible to you.

🎯 How to Claim a Task

  1. Go to Browse Tasks in the sidebar.
  2. Filter by category, status, or sort by deadline or points.
  3. Click any task card to open the detail view.
  4. Click Claim Task — the TAT clock starts immediately.
  5. When done, return and click Mark as Complete.

🎚️ Task Difficulty Guide

🟢 Easy
Routine, clearly defined tasks. Ideal for building streaks and quick wins. Low base points but fast to complete.
🟡 Medium
Requires some investigation or cross-referencing policy. Moderate time investment with a solid point reward.
🔴 Hard
Complex, high-stakes tasks with tight deadlines. High point value. Read the description carefully before claiming.

📢 Task Target Types

TEAM Visible to everyone on the team. First-come, first-served or open for all to complete.
SPECIFIC Assigned directly to you by your manager. Only you can see and complete it.
GROUP Assigned to a subset of agents. Only those agents can see and claim it.
ANNOUNCEMENT Read-only broadcast from your manager. Acknowledge it to earn points — no other action needed.

Kudos System

📝 How Kudos Works

  1. Go to Submit Kudos in the sidebar.
  2. Fill in the Case ID, Channel, Issue Type, and your resolution summary.
  3. Explain clearly why this case deserves recognition (min. 20 chars).
  4. Submit — your manager will review it in the Kudos Queue.
  5. If approved, bonus points are added to your account automatically.
  6. If rejected, you'll receive a notification with the reviewer's note.

✅ Tips for Approval

✔️Be specific — include the case ID and what made it stand out.
✔️Describe the outcome — how was the customer's issue resolved or handled?
❌Don't submit vague entries like "I helped a customer" — these get rejected.
❌Don't submit the same case ID twice — duplicates are rejected.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I earn points? ›
Points are earned by completing tasks, earning early-completion bonuses, being the first to finish a task, and getting Kudos approved by your manager. Demerits will deduct points. Your total is always visible on your Profile page.
I claimed a task by mistake. What do I do? ›
Contact your manager directly and ask them to unclaim the task on your behalf via the Task Manager. Do not leave it idle — unclaimed tasks that go past their deadline become expired and may result in a demerit.
Why can't I see certain tasks? ›
Some tasks are assigned to specific agents or groups only. If a task was assigned directly to another agent (Specific target type) or to a group you're not part of (Group target type), it won't appear in your feed. Check with your manager if you think you're missing assignments.
When does the monthly leaderboard reset? ›
Monthly points reset on the 1st of each month. Your All-Time Points and tier progression never reset — they are cumulative across all months and reflect your total contribution to the team.
My points look wrong. How do I report it? ›
Use the Submit Feedback option (sidebar or the button below) and select Bug Report. Include your LDAP, the approximate date, and the task or Kudos involved. Your manager can investigate and manually adjust points if needed.
Can I see my demerit history? ›
Yes. Navigate to My Profile and scroll down to the Demerit Log section. It shows the type, date, points deducted, and the manager who issued it. If you believe a demerit was issued in error, raise it with your manager directly.
